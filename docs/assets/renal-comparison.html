You askI always mix up nephritic and nephrotic syndrome.

Start with what the glomerulus is losing.

Nephritic syndrome

Inflammation + blood

Inflammatory damage↓Red cells escape;
filtration falls↓Blood in the urine,
salt and water retention

Nephrotic syndrome

Heavy protein loss

Albumin leaks into urine↓Plasma oncotic
pressure falls↓Fluid collects
in the tissues

The exam trapBoth can cause edema. Proteinuria can overlap too.
Swelling alone does not distinguish them.
